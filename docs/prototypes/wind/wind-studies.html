Wind, six ways

Today the wind is one line in the Right-now strip and a quick-fact tile with nothing under its sentence. These are six options for what could go there, all drawn from the station's real archive. Recent means the last 7 days on record; seasonal means every winter since 2023.

A · Compass quick-fact tile

Shows: direction now, the last hour's directions as dots on the rim, and the usual winter direction as a band on the ring.Good for: the quick-fact tile. It fills the empty space and answers "from where" at a glance.Watch: the instant reading is often 0 mph while the 10-minute average is not. The compass uses the 10-minute fields.
B · Wind rose, recent against seasonal in-context tile

Shows: how often the wind came from each of 16 directions in the last 7 days, stacked by speed. The dashed outline is the chosen season across all years. Calm readings sit in the centre circle.Good for: the "usually from" claim, and the Gusts switch shows gusts come from the same quarter.Watch: calm is 21–33% of readings here. The rose leaves calm out of the petals so it does not shrink them.
C · Speed against gusts, last 24 hours in-context tile

The bold line is the 15-minute average speed. Each tick rises to the strongest gust in those 15 minutes. The band is the middle half of winter gusts for each hour. Arrows along the top point downwind, one per hour.

Good for: showing that gusts here run about twice the average speed, and when the gusty spells came.Watch: with readings every 5 minutes, ticks for single readings crowd a phone. The chart groups them into 15 minutes.
D · Today's top gust against winter days range bar

Shows: the top gust of each winter day. The whisker runs from the 10th to the 90th percentile, the bar is the middle half, and the rule marked now is today.Good for: a "breezy for the season" claim in the same shape as the temperature range bars.
