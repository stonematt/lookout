The days switch is the 30 / 90 segmented control over a chart of days: pills in a `rule-strong` border, 44px tall on a phone and 40px from 760px, with the pressed one an `ink` fill with `ground` text and weight 600.

**Consumer provides.** The two options and which is pressed. On a phone the default is the last 30 days; desktop shows 90.

**Use.** Mark the pressed segment with `aria-pressed`. Where a choice has more than three options or would not fit a phone row, use a native `select` styled as a pill instead (the water-year filter). Keyboard focus is a 2px `ink` ring drawn inside the segment.

Static rendition of `web/src/charts/days-switch.ts` and `days-switch.css`.
