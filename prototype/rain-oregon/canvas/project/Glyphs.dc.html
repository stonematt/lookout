Is it a storm or a shower?

One mark per event on the Rain Events list, four categories, one colour. The word stays beside it for the screen reader and the first read.

A · Drop count

one to four drops
sprinkle
shower
rain
storm

Reads as weather, not size. Four drops is not four times the rain.

B · Four steps

filled to the category
sprinkle
shower

storm

Reads as a size and lines up on every row. One colour. Recommended.

a disc in a ring
sprinkle
shower

storm

Reads as size too, but the small sizes get tiny and the rows do not align as well.
