Q1 · The meter's two windows

7 d + 30 d keeps a story on dry days; 24 h + 7 d goes blank on half of winter

Lookout Post, Dec–Feb back-test: the 24 h window has no wet hours on 49% of days. The 7 d window has a median of about 18 wet hours of 168 and a 90th percentile of about 57. Countable hours: a week with an 18-hour gap

The label reads wet hours of countable hours. The 18 hatched hours are missing, not dry: they sit at the end of the bar and are never counted in the second number.

Why 7 d + 30 d

The issue's season question is "this week and this month". The last few hours already belong to the now story in the feature tile, so a 24 h bar repeats it, and holds 0 h on half of winter's days. With 7 d + 30 d the meter still says something on day 12 of a dry spell: the month was wet, the week was not.

What 24 h + 7 d does better

On a storm day the 24 h bar is three-quarters full (18 h of 24), the most vivid picture of "it's pouring today". That picture is already the feature tile's job while rain leads. The cost is the empty 24 h bar on dry winter days.
